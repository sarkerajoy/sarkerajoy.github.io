About Me

Engineer & Researcher with a focus on innovative materials and computational mechanics.

Ajoy Kumar Sarker

I'm a passionate mechanical engineer and a prospecting PhD student with a focus on innovative materials and bio-engineering. My academic journey has provided me with deep expertise in mechanical design, materials science, and advanced manufacturing processes. I've worked on optimizing Functionally Graded Materials (FGMs), with hands-on experience in CAD software, and process optimization.

I've also been part of competitive teams like the BUET Mars Rover Team (Team Interplanetar), where I contributed to the mechanical sub-team, helping us achieve 8th place globally at the European Rover Challenge 2022. Recently, I've expanded my skills into the realm of machine learning, integrating these techniques into research and projects.

I'm always looking to push the boundaries of engineering and technology, whether it's through research, design, or exploring new learning opportunities.
